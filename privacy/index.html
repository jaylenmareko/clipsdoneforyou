Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information ClipsDoneForYou collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and account credentials (passwords are stored hashed; we never store them in plain text).
  • Connected channels: when you connect a social media channel, our publishing partner Upload-Post provides us with access tokens and basic channel information (such as channel name and ID) through the platform's official authorization flow. We never receive or store your social media passwords.
  • Your content: the videos and video links you provide or select, and the clips, titles, and descriptions our service generates from them.
  • Activity data: records of your clips and publications, including titles, timestamps, plan usage, and resulting post links.
  • Billing information: subscriptions are sold and processed by Paddle as merchant of record. Paddle collects your payment details directly; we receive only subscription status, plan, and customer/subscription identifiers. We do not store full payment card numbers.
  • Technical data: basic logs and device/browser information needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use the information above to operate the service: editing your videos into clips, publishing them to your connected channels as you direct, enforcing plan limits, processing subscriptions, providing support, preventing abuse, and improving the service. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your content for advertising.

Sharing with service providers

We share information only with the providers needed to run the service: Upload-Post (publishing to your connected channels), Paddle (payments and subscription management), and our hosting and database providers. When a clip is published, the destination platform (for example, YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram) receives the content through its own API under its own privacy policy. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can disconnect a channel at any time, which revokes our publishing access. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact support@clipsdoneforyou.com and we will delete it within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate billing records.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and restricted access to credentials and tokens. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies

We use only the cookies and local storage needed to keep you signed in and operate the service. We do not use advertising trackers.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@clipsdoneforyou.com
