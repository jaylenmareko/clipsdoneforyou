Refund Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy explains how refunds and cancellations work for ClipsDoneForYou. If you have any questions, email us at support@clipsdoneforyou.com.

Subscriptions (Solo and Multi)

  • You can cancel your subscription at any time. When you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and then it does not renew.
  • We do not provide refunds for partial billing periods — if you cancel mid-period, you keep access until that period ends but are not refunded for the unused time.

Done-for-you setup ($299 one-time)

  • The one-time $299 done-for-you setup is refundable only if work has not yet started. Once we have begun the setup work, the fee is non-refundable.

Overage clips ($0.25 each)

  • Per-clip overage charges ($0.25 per clip) are non-refundable once the clip has been posted.

How to cancel

You can cancel your subscription from the Billing section of your account at any time. Cancelling returns your account to the free plan at the end of your paid period.

Questions about a charge or refund? Contact us at support@clipsdoneforyou.com and we'll help.
