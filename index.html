Questions

Do I have to edit anything?

No. Clipping, titles, and posting are done for you.

How do I change things?

Just say it in the chat — "post 5 a day", "make TikTok clips shorter", "clip this video".

What formats?

Shorts on every plan. ~10-minute cuts on Multi and up. Lengths are set per channel.

What happens after 10 free clips?

Posting pauses, your clips stay banked, and subscribing picks up right where it left off.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No lock-in.
